About

How Hallucination Hunter checks AI-generated text and what it does with your data.

Language models write in the same confident tone whether they are stating a well-known fact or inventing a number. Hallucination Hunter breaks an answer into single claims so you can see which lines hold up and which need a second look.

How a check runs

  1. Split into claims. Your text is broken into standalone factual statements, keeping exact numbers, dates, and names.
  2. Verify each statement. Each claim is tested against established facts and given a 0–100 confidence score. Anything uncertain or post-cutoff is marked unverifiable rather than guessed.
  3. Attach sources. Every verdict includes an explanation, a correction when false, and a link you can inspect.
  4. Underline inline. Claims are highlighted directly inside your original passage so errors are easy to spot.

Privacy

Checked text is sent through a serverless API route to Groq and stored in Supabase Postgres under a random browser ID. There is no account wall, tracking cookie, or analytics script. Share links encode the report directly in the URL hash.
