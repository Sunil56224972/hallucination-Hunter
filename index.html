Claim-by-claim ledger

  1. Atomic claim isolation

    A language model reads the passage and decomposes it into discrete assertions that can stand or fall independently. Subjective prose, rhetorical framing, and hedged opinions are set aside; exact figures, dates, and names are preserved verbatim.

  2. Adversarial verification

    Each isolated statement is tested against established reference consensus. Rather than guessing on ambiguous or recent claims, the verifier is instructed to return “unverifiable” and to supply the exact corrected figure whenever a statement fails.

  3. Confidence weighting & attribution

    Every verdict receives a certainty rating from 0 to 100 alongside a primary reference link. Your overall Verification Index reflects the proportion of claims corroborated above doubt, with anything beneath your Settings threshold flagged.

Proofreader’s Key

What the editorial stamps mean

Verified

Corroborated by record

Supported by authoritative primary sources with high certainty. Open the linked reference to read the underlying record directly.

Unverifiable

Open editorial query

Insufficient consensus either way. Not proven false, but unsuited for publication or decisions without independent verification.

Contradicted

Refuted by primary facts

Directly contradicted by established evidence. The card supplies a corrected statement alongside the refuting citation.

Editorial note: treat automated verification as a rigorous second pair of eyes, not final legal or medical counsel. Always inspect the primary source.

About Hallucination Hunter

Language models write with identical composure whether they are citing established record or inventing plausible fiction. Hallucination Hunter dissects an answer one assertion at a time so you can see exactly which sentences earn your trust.

Verification workflow

  1. Isolation. The passage is decomposed into self-contained factual claims with exact numbers and dates preserved.
  2. Cross-examination. Each statement is evaluated against authoritative literature and scored from 0 to 100.
  3. Attribution. Every verdict links to a verifiable reference organization or primary page.
  4. Galley mark-up. The original manuscript is underlined in place so fabrications stand out at a glance.

Privacy & data

Passages submitted for checking are processed via the serverless verification proxy and logged alongside an anonymous browser identifier in Supabase Postgres. No sign-up, cookies, or third-party analytics scripts run on this page. Share links encode verdicts directly inside the URL fragment.
